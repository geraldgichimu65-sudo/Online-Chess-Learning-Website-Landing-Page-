Meet GM Igor Smirnov

♔
FIDE Grandmaster Rated 2500+

"Most players study chess their whole lives and never improve — because nobody taught them how to study."

— GM Igor Smirnov

Igor Smirnov earned his International Master title at just 13 years old — and his Grandmaster title at 20. He didn't stop there. After reaching the elite levels of competitive chess, Igor turned his attention to a harder problem: why do most chess players never break through their rating ceiling?

The answer, he found, wasn't talent. It was method. Most club players study openings, memorise tactics, and grind bullet games — but never develop the strategic thinking that separates a 1400 from a 2000. Igor spent years reverse-engineering the Grandmaster thought process into a system anyone could follow.

13 Age at IM title
20 Age at GM title

130+ Countries reached

In 2012, Igor founded the Remote Chess Academy — now one of the world's most followed chess education platforms. His students have gone on to win national championships, earn FIDE titles, and — more importantly to Igor — finally start enjoying the game again.

Featured in The Hindu and recognised internationally, Igor has given simultaneous exhibitions on 10 boards and coached players across every continent. His methods have been called "the missing manual for adult chess improvement."
